# Global-Unicorn-Companies
# Dashboard Overview

This Excel dashboard provides an interactive analysis of global unicorn companies using key business and investment metrics. It consolidates company-level data into visual charts and summaries, enabling quick insights into valuation trends, funding patterns, industry distribution, and geographical presence.

# Key Insights Covered
Country-wise Distribution – Shows the number of unicorn companies across different countries.
Year-wise Incorporation – Analyses companies based on their founding period.
Industry Analysis – Displays the distribution of unicorns across various industries.
Top 10 Companies by Valuation – Highlights the highest-valued unicorn companies.
Funding Analysis – Tracks total funds raised by companies and compares funding trends across industries.
Investment Range – Classifies companies according to the amount of capital raised.
Investor Analysis – Shows the relationship between investor count and company growth.
Financial Stage Analysis – Examines funding trends based on different financing stages.
Country-wise Trends – Compares unicorn growth and investment activity across countries.
Purpose

The dashboard is designed to provide decision-makers with a quick, visual overview of the global unicorn ecosystem, helping identify investment trends, emerging industries, geographic hotspots, and high-value companies. It supports data-driven analysis through interactive charts and consolidated performance metrics.
